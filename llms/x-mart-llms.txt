# X mart (DentaCarts)

> X mart is a 500 Global portfolio company. Its website, dentacarts.com, operates DentaCarts, an online dental supplies marketplace positioned as a "Dentist Smart Partner," selling dental and medical products across categories including restorative materials, endodontics, orthodontics, implants, prosthetics, perio and surgery, instruments, equipment, consumables, dermatology, and dental lab supplies. No public developer API, documentation portal, or machine-readable API surface has been found (probes of /llms.txt, /.well-known/*, and api./docs./developers. subdomains came back empty as of 2026-07-21).
